## ✨ What Has Been Upgraded

### 1. Realistic, Organic 3D Floating Archipelago
- **Procedural Sculpted Rock Landmasses (`OrganicIslandMesh.tsx`)**:
  - Replaced the previous flat geometric discs and spiky cones with organic, naturally undulating terrain.
  - Multi-tiered grassy plateau top with mossy contours.
  - Craggy dark slate underside (`#1e2430`) with organic striations, stone overhangs, and stalactites tapering down into the clouds.
  - **Glowing Golden Rock Cave Windows**: Arched window niches carved directly into the craggy rock faces casting warm ambient light on the cliffs (matching Reference Image 5).
  - Draping green ivy vines and hanging moss tendrils dripping over the edges.

- **Flowering Cherry Blossom Trees (`FantasyTree3D.tsx`)**:
  - Gnarled natural wood trunks and branches with gentle wind-sway physics.
  - Volumetric puffy cloud clusters of cherry blossom pink petals (`#ff9ebb` / `#ffc2d1`) framing the pagoda cottages.
  - Scattered fallen petals on the mossy grass.

- **Volumetric Rushing Waterfalls (`RealisticWaterfall3D.tsx`)**:
  - Curved 3D water curtains pouring over the rocky rim with translucent turquoise flow, shimmering specular highlights, splash foam rings, and rising mist particles.
  - 3 distinct waterfall streams on the central island, plus vertical cascades on satellite islands.

- **Realistic Curved Blooming Lotus Flowers (`LotusFlowers3D.tsx`)**:
  - Replaced all cone primitives with smooth, multi-layered curved petals (outer flared, mid opening, inner cupped) with luminous translucency in pink, magenta, sunset orange, and cyan.
  - Broad circular green lilypads with characteristic notch resting on the grass and water rim.
  - Golden seed pod and stamen center with warm starlight glow.

- **Celestial Atmosphere & Double Moons (`DoubleMoonsAndSky3D.tsx`)**:
  - Deep twilight cerulean sky gradient (`#0d2847` to `#194a7a`), glowing cyan lunar halos, soft moonlit clouds, and bottom turquoise lake plane.
  - Flying fairy characters with translucent glowing wings and fluttering golden butterflies.
